Our Story

A seat of learning older than the nation it helped build

Founded on 1 January 1864 under Dr. G. W. Leitner, Government College began in a haveli of Raja Dhian Singh before moving to its iconic Gothic building in 1877. It was granted university status in 2002 and today serves over 14,000 students across science, humanities and the arts.

Mission

To nurture inquisitive, ethical and courageous minds through rigorous teaching, research and service to Pakistan.

Vision

To be a globally recognised research university rooted in its heritage and committed to the public good.
